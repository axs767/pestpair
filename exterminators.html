How to join

Three steps.

1

Show your Texas license

Your license is checked against Texas Department of Agriculture records before you can take jobs.

2

Set your area and hours

Choose the ZIP codes you serve and when you are available. Customers see your estimated arrival time.

3

Accept jobs and earn reviews

Start in Fast, where solo exterminators compete on price and arrival time. Build reviews to move up to Trusted.

What you keep

PestPair takes 15% of each job. You keep the other 85%.
